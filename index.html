Predicted goals, assists and ratings

For each remaining match, the model's expected goals for a team are shared among its expected XI by each player's xG (and xA for assists). Ratings come from a model fitted on real 2024-26 ratings: goals, assists, results and clean sheets, by position. "Predicted" is the stable expected total; "This season" is the played-out season above and changes on re-roll.

An independent fan project, not affiliated with the Premier League or any club. One played-out season is one possibility, not a forecast of exact scores; the win/draw/loss chances on each card are the model's real probabilities.

Model: blend of three Poisson goals models trained on 2024-25 to 2026-27 matches (team form, managers, expected-XI player stats). Data: openfootball, FPL archive, FPL-Elo-Insights, Wikipedia.
